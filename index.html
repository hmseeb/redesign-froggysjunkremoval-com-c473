About Froggy's

A local neighbor you can actually count on

Hi, I'm Tony, the owner of Froggy's Junk Removal. I'm a proud Marine Corps veteran, husband, and dad of four. After seeing how quickly the East Valley is growing, I realized there was a real need for a junk removal company that people could truly count on — one that's honest, affordable, reliable, and treats customers like neighbors, not just another job.

I started Froggy's Junk Removal to help homeowners, landlords, property managers, and local businesses clear out unwanted items without the stress. Whether it's a single item, a garage cleanout, a tenant move-out, or a full property cleanout, my goal is to make the process simple from start to finish.

As a locally owned family business, every customer matters to us. Unlike large national franchises, we don't have expensive franchise fees or corporate overhead to pass on to you. That means competitive pricing with the personal service, flexibility, and attention to detail only a local owner-operated business can offer. When you call Froggy's, you're talking directly to the owner — not a call center.

When you choose Froggy's Junk Removal, you're supporting a local family, investing back into your community, and working with someone who genuinely cares about earning your trust. We proudly serve Queen Creek, San Tan Valley, Gilbert, East Mesa, and the surrounding East Valley communities.

We look forward to helping you reclaim your space!

FAQs

Good questions, straight answers

Still wondering about something? Call or text (480) 331-1842 — you'll reach Tony directly.

What types of junk do you remove?

We handle a wide variety of junk, including furniture, appliances, electronics, yard waste, and construction debris. If you have something you need removed, just ask!

Do you offer same-day service?

Yes! We pride ourselves on our quick response times and often provide same-day or next-day service to accommodate your needs.

Is your team insured?

Absolutely! We are fully insured, ensuring that you can have peace of mind knowing that your property is in safe hands during the junk removal process.

What is your minimum fee?

We offer single-item pricing on certain items starting as low as $120.00.

Do you give free estimates?

Yes! Fill out the form above or text us pictures of your junk for a ballpark quote — or we can come out and take a look at no obligation.

Do I need to have everything in one big pile?

No. We can remove items from anywhere on your property, including inside your home, garage, attic, or backyard. However, if your junk is already gathered in one easily accessible pile outside, your removal cost may be lower since it reduces the time and labor required to collect individual items.
